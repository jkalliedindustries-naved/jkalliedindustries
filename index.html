About Us

ISO 9001:2015 Certified Manufacturer & Exporter

JK Allied Industries, founded by Naved Khan, is an ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer, exporter and stockist based in Bankra, Howrah, West Bengal (711403). We manufacture complete HT & LT transmission line hardware including Gang AB Switches (200A to 1250A), DO Fuse Sets, Horn Gap Switches, Brass & Aluminium Metal Castings, Hardware Fittings, Stay Set accessories and Overhead Line Materials.

Our firm has supplied complete accessories to various State Electricity Boards and DISCOMs across India as per their specifications. We are continuously developing new products for distribution line materials.
